# Browse rooms and availability

## Purpose

Staff see the rooms and which one-hour slots are free or taken, today and on later days.

## User Stories

- F1.1 As a staff member, I see the list of rooms with each room's name, capacity and location (floor/building).
- F1.2 As a staff member, I pick a day, from today up to 30 days ahead, and see each room's one-hour slots from 08:00 to 18:00 as free or taken.
- F1.3 As a staff member, I see who booked each taken slot.
- F1.4 As a staff member, I see that slots already past today are not available.
- F1.5 As a facilities admin, I see the same rooms and availability, including who booked each taken slot.

## Decisions

- Bookable hours are 08:00 to 18:00 every day, in one-hour slots.
- Staff can view and book up to 30 days ahead.
- A room shows its name, capacity and location; no equipment details.
